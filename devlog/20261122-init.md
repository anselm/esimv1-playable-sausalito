# 2026-11-22 — playable-sausalito: init

Scaffolded from the harness templates on 2026-11-22. Point-in-time entry.

## Why this project exists

Anselm wants a playable model of Sausalito: a simulation of near-term futures of the
town that residents and local government can both use, where a person sees an issue,
tries an idea and gets a feel for what follows. It is kept in its own public repository
so that esimv1 is used as a simulation core from outside, the way a library is used.

## Grounding

- Ask, in his words (22 November 2026, parts that concern the build):
  - "my main goal is to build a playable sausalito. it can be a separate but public
    repo. a separate repo begins to help us exercise esimv1 as a simulation core,
    package or service - someday used by thousands of developers - similar to say
    threejs. this also implies that we will move the other projects out of esimv1 at
    some point soon also."
  - "today we are just sketching however. effectively telling the story - that we can
    make a playable sausalito"
  - On the term: "i was thinking 'playable place' - riffing slightly on bret victor
    'explorable explanations' ... one blemish in the term is that i think a city or a
    town can become an intelligent entity or agent, speaking for itself"
  - On the bridge: "some kind of web service that bridges between local government and
    lay person interests ... where citizens can see issues, try out different ideas, and
    get a real feel for downstream impacts ... understanding where the fabric of
    community 'tears' versus stretches."
  - On play: "the user will be at a certain point in time, set conditions, hit play,
