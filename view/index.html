Ground: US Geological Survey 3DEP one-metre lidar elevation, 2018 survey, drawn at 4 m. Trees: whatever stands 3 m or more above the ground outside buildings in the same survey's points (US Geological Survey), one body per 7 m of canopy. Roads are OpenStreetMap's lines drawn as geometry on the ground, as wide as their lanes on the map or as their class usually is; bridges stand at the height the lidar saw. Buildings, roads and piers: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Building heights: . Clock time is Pacific Standard Time. Water: a flat surface at the level of NOAA's San Francisco tide gauge (9414290), measured or predicted. Ground below that level shows wet whether or not the bay can reach it; walls, drains and pumps are not in the picture. The waves are decoration scaled by the measured wind. Piers are drawn as if all of them float. Sky: the nearest National Weather Service observation. Red pins: places named in the news the inhaler has read.
